Product Updates

HotelDesk is actively taking shape.

This page tracks meaningful progress in the HotelDesk product — from workflow refinement to operational structure and interface direction. The goal is simple: build a system that feels clear, modern, and practical in real hotel use.

Recent Progress

What has been refined so far.

These updates focus on the parts of HotelDesk that directly affect hotel workflow, staff clarity, and management visibility.

March 2026 · Reception workflow refined

The reception experience was sharpened around room visibility, guest action, and fast movement between check-in, check-out, and cleaning. This keeps front desk interaction focused instead of buried in menus.

March 2026 · Room state system clarified

Room states were structured more clearly across the product: Available, Occupied, Cleaning, Maintenance, Reserved, and Checkout Soon. This improves operational awareness and reduces room-status confusion.

March 2026 · Check-in and payment flow structured

The check-in flow was improved to better handle guest details, payment method, payment type, and balance visibility. This makes payment situations easier to understand at the point of entry.

March 2026 · Check-out to cleaning handoff defined

HotelDesk now follows a clearer operational transition from guest check-out into pending cleaning, then ready state. This is important for keeping room readiness visible and reducing staff mismatch.

March 2026 · Admin visibility strengthened

The admin side was shaped around summary cards, stay records, receipts, user management, and activity monitoring. The goal is to give management a calm but clear view of what is happening across the hotel.

March 2026 · Receipt ID and accountability logic added

A structured receipt ID concept was introduced to support transaction traceability, receipt reference, and cleaner record keeping. This improves accountability and future receipt retrieval.

March 2026 · Activity logging concept expanded

HotelDesk now more clearly tracks the idea of significant staff and system actions through activity logs. This supports monitoring, auditing, and operational transparency.

March 2026 · Marketing website structure launched

The public-facing HotelDesk website structure was expanded to include the homepage, product page, pricing, about page, and updates page. This gives potential clients a clearer introduction to what HotelDesk is and where it is heading.

Why This Matters

This is not random motion.

Every update is tied to one idea: make hotel operations easier to understand, easier to manage, and easier to trust.

HotelDesk is being shaped around real workflow logic, not bloated software ambition. That means progress is measured by clarity, usefulness, and practical structure.
